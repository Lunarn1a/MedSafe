## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished standalone logo symbol for MedSafe, a medication safety assistant website. A bold, softly rounded shield enclosing one clearly recognizable diagonal medicine capsule; integrate the capsule elegantly with the shield using clean negative space. Minimal flat vector-like design, crisp smooth edges, balanced proportions, strong silhouette readable at small sidebar icon sizes. Use a restrained deep teal and dark charcoal palette appropriate for a calm healthcare interface. Square composition, symbol centered and occupying most of the canvas with modest even padding. Genuinely transparent background. Symbol only: no text, no lettering, no tagline, no watermark, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no medical cross. Deliver a single finished logo mark, not a sheet of variations.
